## Official problem statement

**Title:** Unlock Underused Equipment and Spaces

**One-line description:** Build a trusted platform that helps institutions and
communities safely share underused spaces, equipment and technical resources.

## Problem description

Colleges, laboratories, makerspaces, community organisations and small businesses
own valuable resources that remain unused for long periods. These include
laboratories, classrooms, meeting rooms, computers, cameras, fabrication tools,
testing devices and specialised facilities.

Meanwhile, students, researchers, creators and early-stage teams often cannot
afford these resources.

Ordinary booking systems do not adequately address institutional trust,
eligibility, safety, human approval, accountability, damage tracking or fair
access.

The platform must enable verified owners to share resources while retaining
control of their property.

## Mandatory end-to-end workflow

1. An owner lists a resource and defines its conditions.
2. A user discovers the resource and checks eligibility.
3. The user submits a booking request.
4. The system verifies applicable requirements.
5. An owner or authorised manager reviews the request.
6. The request is approved, rejected or waitlisted.
7. The user checks in and documents initial condition.
8. The resource is returned and final condition recorded.
9. Delays, damage or conflicts are reported and handled.
10. Both parties can view the complete transaction history.

The final application must allow a judge to complete at least one full lifecycle
without assistance.

## Expected capabilities

- Resource listings with availability, location, conditions and usage rules.
- Search, filtering or intelligent matching.
- User and owner profiles with appropriate verification.
- Eligibility checks for training, qualifications and membership.
- Booking approval, rejection and waiting lists.
- Transparent handling of competing requests.
- Check-in and check-out.
- Before-and-after condition reports.
- Damage, delay and incident reporting.
- Approval, cancellation and overdue notifications.
- Fair usage limits preventing monopolisation.
- Role-based access control.
- Auditable history.
- Privacy and security controls.

AI is optional. Important access, safety and penalty decisions must remain
reviewable by authorised humans.

## Official submission requirements

- Publicly accessible working application.
- Accessible source-code repository.
- Project PDF using the official format.
- Selected use case and target users.
- Problem understanding and supporting reasoning.
- Proposed solution and complete workflow.
- Completed and incomplete features.
- Technology stack and architecture.
- Testing notes and judge instructions.
- Security, privacy and fairness considerations.
- Future scope.
- Team and participant details.
- Each member's real role and contribution.
- Disclosure of libraries, templates, pre-existing code and AI assistance.

Every team member submits individually.

## Rules

- The original product must be built within the official 48-hour development window.
- Existing frameworks, libraries, APIs and development tools are allowed.
- Pre-existing code/assets and significant AI usage must be disclosed.
- Plagiarism and misleading submissions are prohibited.
- Demonstration data must be fictional, anonymised or used with permission.
- Restricted resources must not become accessible without authorisation.
- Important safety decisions require human oversight.
- Links must remain accessible throughout judging.
- Judges may examine Git history and development evidence.
